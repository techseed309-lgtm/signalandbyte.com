Our standards

Sourced, checked, honest

Every video ends with something you can use, and nothing in it is there just to keep you watching.

  • Sources linked. Every claim in a video is listed with its source in the description.
  • The other side, too. We put the strongest counter-argument on screen before we conclude.
  • Corrections in public. If we get something wrong, we fix it and say so in the description.
  • No hype, no get-rich-quick. If something only worked once, we say that.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Where do your facts come from?

From the original source wherever possible: research papers, company and lab posts, official filings, changelogs and documentation. Every source is linked in the video's description.

I think you got something wrong. What do I do?

Tell us at hello@signalandbyte.com with the video and the timestamp. If we were wrong, we correct it publicly in the description.

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes, please. Leave a comment under any video or email hello@signalandbyte.com. The best ideas come with a link to the source.

Do you take sponsorships?

Yes, for products our viewers would actually use, always clearly labelled. Write to business@signalandbyte.com.
