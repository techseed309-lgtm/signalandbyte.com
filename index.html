Our standards

Sourced, checked, honest

Every video ends with something you can use, and nothing in it is there just to keep you watching.

  • Sources linked. Every claim in a video is listed with its source in the description.
  • The other side, too. We put the strongest counter-argument on screen before we conclude.
  • Corrections in public. If we get something wrong, we fix it and say so in the description.
  • No hype, no get-rich-quick. If something only worked once, we say that.
