Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or ads, and has no forms or sign-ups.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes (see GitHub's privacy statement). Fonts load from Google Fonts, and video thumbnails load from YouTube's image servers. A video in an article loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) only when you press play. Share and follow buttons are plain links: nothing is sent to those services until you click one, and then their own privacy policies apply.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply. We never add you to a mailing list or share your address.
